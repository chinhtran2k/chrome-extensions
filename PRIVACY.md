# Privacy Policy

**Page to Markdown — Clean Copy for AI**
**Table to CSV — Merged Cells Done Right**

Last updated: 17 September 2026

## The short version

Neither extension collects, transmits or stores your data. There is no server
to send anything to.

## What the extensions do with page content

When you click the extension button (or press its keyboard shortcut), the
extension reads the content of the page you are currently viewing, converts it,
and gives the result back to you — on your clipboard, or as a file you save.

That conversion happens entirely inside your browser. The page content is held
in memory only for as long as the conversion takes, and is never written to a
server, a log, or any storage we control.

Neither extension can read a page until you ask it to. Both request only the
`activeTab` permission, which grants access to a single tab at the moment you
click, rather than standing access to every site you visit.

## What is stored

Your own settings — whether to keep links, images or tables, your chosen export
format, and similar preferences — are saved using Chrome's `storage` API. These
stay in your browser profile and sync through your own Google account if you
have Chrome sync enabled. We cannot see them.

Table to CSV also stores a licence key if you enter one. It is verified locally,
on your machine, and is not sent anywhere.

## What is not collected

- No personal information
- No browsing history
- No page content
- No analytics, telemetry or usage statistics
- No advertising identifiers
- No cookies

## Third parties

Neither extension contacts any third-party service, loads any remote code, or
includes any third-party library.

Table to CSV can open an export link on a site you are already using — for
example Google's own CSV export for a Google Sheet you have open. That opens in
a normal browser tab under your existing session, and no data passes through us.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this same
address with a new date at the top.
